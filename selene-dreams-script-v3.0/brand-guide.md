# Selene Dreams — Brand Guide (AUTHORITATIVE)

Source: *SELENE DREAMS NAA Brand Guidelines* (41pp, agency NAA, Jan 2025).
Deck map: Branding pp.4–6 · Colors pp.8–11 · Typography pp.13–17 ·
Photography pp.19–22 · Brand Voice pp.24–27 · Composition pp.30–32 ·
Packaging pp.34–39.

**This file is the authority for every agent in the pipeline.** The visual
screener scores references against it, the prompt writer builds generation
prompts from it, the QA reviewer scores generated images against it, and the
caption reviewer checks voice against it. Where it conflicts with any other
instruction — including an agent's own defaults — this document wins.

---

## Essence

Luxury natural-fibre bedding.

**Mission:** the bed is "the anchor to your home… and also a portal to places
far, far away."

**Promise:** "Selene sheets feel right at home, but they take you far away."

**Tagline:** *Where do you go when you dream?*

**We are:** verdant · serene · strikingly soft · natural · transportative ·
ethereal · breezy · intimate · imaginative · grounding · unconfined ·
uncomplicated.

**We are not:** rough · restrictive · mechanical · artificial · industrial ·
harsh · coarse · flimsy · uninviting.

---

## Voice

Feminine sensibility. "Soft words." Mix practical, direct sentences with
whimsical, ethereal ones — the practicality keeps it credible, the whimsy
keeps it Selene.

Sample lines from the deck:

- "Achieve goddess-level sleep"
- "Sleep with Selene"
- "Take me to bed (as long as it has good sheets)"

Never hypey, never exclamation-stacked, never meme-voice, never discount-brand
urgency.

## Colour

**Primary:** Moss Green `#273F22` (the warm alternative to black) · Ecru
`#E8E4D8` · Winter White `#F5F3EF`

**Secondary:** Sage Green `#7C896F` · Wedgewood Dark `#335875` · Blue Light
`#B0CEEC` · Rose des Sables `#F7D4BD` · Apricot Medium `#FF6F55`

**Rule:** never show materials on a powerful or dark background. Product sits
on neutrals; dark and powerful colours are reserved for text.

---

## Typography

- **EK Roumald Mono** — logotype only, all caps, stamp feel.
- **EK Roumald** — titles, uppercase, ≤12 words. Italic for the tagline.
- **Inter** — all body copy.
- Brand icon: asterisk-flower ✱. All shapes rounded (corner radius 15).

## Photography — the operational core

**The signature is bedding OUTDOORS IN WILD NATURE.** A bed on a meadow.
Folded stacks on grass. A mattress on a shore. Sheets on a clothesline in a
field. Fog forests, dunes, cotton-grass. The bed as a portal to a dream world.

**Two shot types:**

1. **Campaign** — natural setting, beds in use, the world around them visible.
2. **Product** — minimal neutral studio, bed centred, craftsmanship close-ups.

**Lighting:** always natural. Preferably the warm glow of dusk or dawn.

**Treatment:** subtle analog film grain, matte grade, rounded or blurred
overlay shapes.

**People:** welcome and encouraged — the feed should feel LIVED-IN, not
like empty showrooms. Aim for a human trace in roughly half of campaign
shots: someone asleep under the sheets, a figure walking away across the
meadow, hands smoothing linen, feet peeking from under a duvet, a silhouette
at dusk. Always ANONYMOUS — asleep, distant, face averted or out of frame;
never a posed model looking at camera. (Updated 2026-08-26: was “used
sparingly” — posts were reading as empty bedroom shots lacking life.)

**Materials in frame:** cotton, linen, canvas, grass, stone, water, wood only.

## The 12-point on-brand image rubric

Score 1 point per rule. **10–12 strong · 7–9 acceptable · ≤6 off-brand.**

1. Bed-in-nature, or a minimal neutral studio
2. Soft natural light, dusk or dawn
3. Serene, dreamlike mood
4. Muted greens / ecru / sea tones + soft pastels — no neon
5. Product shown on neutral ground only
6. Film grain / matte grade — no HDR
7. Signs of life welcome; any people anonymous (asleep / distant / face averted)
8. Natural materials only
9. Fabric softness is the hero
10. Rounded geometry in any graphic elements
11. Brand typography if text is present
12. Reads verdant / ethereal / intimate — fails if industrial or harsh

## Instant excludes

Regardless of engagement, these never reach a shortlist and are never
generated: giveaways and contests · dorm or back-to-school staging · busy
commercial product line-ups · urban or industrial settings · neon or
oversaturated grades · meme-voice posts.

## Note for the generation pipeline (2026-08-26)

People policy loosened from “sparingly” to “welcome, ~half of campaign
shots carry a human trace.” Anonymity rule unchanged. Screener: do not
penalise a reference for containing people if they are anonymous; DO
favour lived-in warmth over empty rooms. Prompt writer: include an
anonymous human element in about every other campaign-style prompt.

## Note for the generation pipeline (2026-08-12)

Earlier prompt-writing defaults described "scandinavian / japandi /
mediterranean indoor bedrooms, no people." Those predate this deck and are
**off-brand relative to it**. The deck's world is outdoor-natural, dusk-lit,
film-grained, with anonymous people permitted.

Where a reference photograph and this guide disagree, borrow the reference's
composition, craft and quality of light — but move the scene into Selene's
world. This document decides what the images look like.
