# Verified product facts — the only ones copy may state

## French Linen
- Made from European flax.
- Processed and woven in Selene's own family facilities.
- Textured and breathable.
- The site's own line: "hollow fibers trap warmth in winter and release heat in summer"
  (brand framing, fine to echo in brand voice; do not present as a measured spec).

## Organic Percale
- 340 thread count. (Product page. NOT 400 — the Fabrics Guide is stale.)
- 100% organic cotton, long-staple.
- One-over-one-under weave.
- Crisp, breathable, matte finish.

## Tencel Lyocell
- Made from eucalyptus wood pulp.
- Closed-loop process, 99% of the solvent recovered and recycled.
- Blended with cotton for durability.

## Gauze Blanket
- 100% Organic Egyptian Cotton.
- Four layers of lightweight muslin.
- Naturally crinkled and pre-washed.
- Site wording: "Soft, breathable, and endlessly versatile."
- Site wording: "just the right amount of warmth without weight." WARMTH WITHOUT
  WEIGHT is the claim. Never describe it as heavy, weighted or dense.
- Site wording: "Perfect for summer nights or year-round layering";
  "Light comfort, all season long."
- Sold Queen and King. It is BED bedding, not a couch throw — do not call it a
  throw or place it on a sofa in copy.
- Colourways: Alabaster White · Soft Maple · Shadow Gray. There is no "oatmeal",
  "beige", "cream" or "natural" colourway. Use a real name or none.
- Care: machine wash cold with like colours, tumble dry low or line dry, do not
  bleach, avoid high heat (it preserves the gauze texture).

## Cooling Comforter
- NAME: the site calls it the COOLING COMFORTER. The product-photo filenames say
  "Cooling_Blanket" — that is an internal filename, not the product name.
- 100% Nylon.
- Engineered with Japanese Arc-Chill fabric.
- Site wording: "Cool, breathable, and silky-smooth."
- Site wording: "wicks away heat on contact to keep you comfortably cool through
  the night."
- Sold Queen and King.
- Colourways: Cream White · Ocean Breeze · Silver Mist.
- Care: machine wash separately, cold, gentle cycle. Tumble dry low. Mild
  detergent. Do not bleach, iron or dry clean.
- NOTE it is a synthetic. Do not sweep it into "natural fibre" or "natural
  materials" copy that covers the linen and cotton lines.

## Mulberry Silk (eye mask, pillowcase)
- "Luxury Mulberry Silk" — that is all the site states. No momme, no grade.

## Guarantee
- "365-day guaranteed comfort" — site wording. Returns accepted within 365 days.
- It is NOT a 365-NIGHT sleep trial. Never write it that way.
- The product pages add: "All returns are subject to a $10 return fee deducted
  from your refund amount." Do not imply free returns.

## Certifications — use the product-page wording or nothing
- OEKO-TEX: product pages say "produced in our OEKO-TEX certified partner facilities".
  Do not upgrade this to "OEKO-TEX certified sheets" — the site is inconsistent and the
  facility claim is the one that appears at the point of purchase.
- GOTS: UNCONFIRMED at product level. Do not state it.

# General knowledge that is allowed, but must be declared
Uncontroversial textile or sleep facts that are not on the site (for example, "heat wears
flax down over time", "your body drops its temperature to fall asleep") may be used in
care and ritual posts. Every such line MUST be listed in the output's "generalClaims"
field so it lands in the row remark and Marcus sees it before approving.

# Never
- Invented numbers, temperatures, percentages or studies.
- Clinical or medical claims.
- Any competitor name.
- **Brand history or founding story of any kind.** No claims about how long the family
  has made bedding, who they supplied before, when the Selene name went on the label,
  what is or is not outsourced, or what the company stands behind. Marcus retired this
  content on 2026-09-09. None of it is on the site, so nothing here can check it. If a
  topic seems to call for it, that topic is wrong, not this rule.
